Lecture Models on Easy, Medium, and Hard Levels

Each difficulty uses one fixed, seeded level shared by all methods. Click a level image for a larger view, or open any replay to inspect the agent's attempt frame by frame.

Interpretation: BFS/DFS/UCS/greedy/A* search platform routes; value/policy iteration solve a small approximate graph MDP; hill climbing and simulated annealing optimize candidate routes; Monte Carlo control trains on seeded episodes; UCB selects actions without using state. Their information and compute differ, so these one-level replays illustrate behavior, not a fair ranking or estimate of generalization.
